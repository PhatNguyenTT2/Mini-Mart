## Decision boundary

- `AUTHOR_OFFICIAL` selects the repository explicitly released by paper authors or linked by the original paper. Framework reimplementations remain alternates and cannot silently replace it.
- `FRAMEWORK_AUTHORITATIVE` selects RecBole only for controls/classical methods without an identified author-official executable source. It does not certify equivalence to another repository’s preprocessing or evaluator.
- `INDEPENDENT` covers the project-native Apriori/Deep/Hybrid conditions. Their current file hashes are evidence locks, not a substitute for a clean commit.
- `REJECTED` candidates and reasons are in `repo_exclusion_log.md`; rejection here is for Stage 1E authority/equivalence, not a general quality judgment.
